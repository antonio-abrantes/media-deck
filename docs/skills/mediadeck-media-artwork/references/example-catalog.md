# Validated example catalog

Use these examples to understand MediaDeck consistency across aspect ratios.
Do not copy their game-specific palettes into unrelated artwork.

## Dragon Ball Z: Kakarot

Folder: `examples/dragon-ball-z-kakarot/`

- `dragon-ball-z-kakarot-launcher-cover.png` — universal launcher cover;
- `dragon-ball-z-kakarot-floppy-35-label.png` — 70 × 52 mm;
- `dragon-ball-z-kakarot-cd-jewel-front.png` — 120 × 120 mm;
- `dragon-ball-z-kakarot-dvd-case-front.png` — 130 × 183 mm.

Demonstrates a warm anime palette: red/orange frame, golden highlight, cyan
technical accent, dark navy separator, and identical identity across portrait,
landscape, square, and tall formats.

Required final capacity lines:

- floppy: `COMPRESSED CORE: 1.44 MB`;
- CD: `OPTICAL CORE: 700 MB`;
- DVD: `OPTICAL CORE: 4.70 GB`.

## Need for Speed: Underground

Folder: `examples/need-for-speed-underground/`

- `need-for-speed-underground-launcher-cover.png` — universal launcher cover;
- `need-for-speed-underground-floppy-35-label.png` — 70 × 52 mm;
- `need-for-speed-underground-cd-jewel-front.png` — 120 × 120 mm.

Demonstrates that the border palette is game-derived rather than fixed:
asphalt black and metallic silver form the structure, neon green is the main
highlight, and electric blue/violet are sparse circuit accents. The orange car
remains the focal subject instead of turning the entire frame orange.

## What remains invariant

- angular/chamfered technical frame;
- nested keylines, circuit traces, nodes, and small technical blocks;
- dark separator around principal artwork;
- restrained print wear;
- compact lower metadata area;
- exact physical canvas and safe-area discipline;
- no photographed media body or case mockup in the primary export.
